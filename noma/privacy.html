Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Noma (“the Application”) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the mobile application.

Noma is a personal journaling and note-taking application. It is not a medical or health application and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Application refers to Noma, the mobile application.
  • Company (“We”, “Us”, “Our”) refers to the individual developer of Noma.
  • Country refers to France.
  • Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data refers to technical information that may be collected automatically when using the Application.
  • You means the individual using the Application.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Anonymous Use

Noma can be used without creating a traditional account. When you first use the Application, an anonymous account is created so that your data can be securely associated with your use of the Application and synchronized with our backend.

You may later choose to link this anonymous account to Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In.

Account Linking with Apple or Google

If you choose to link your account using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, we may receive certain information from the authentication provider, depending on the provider and your settings.

This information may include:

  • Email address
  • Authentication identifiers provided by Apple or Google

Noma does not require you to provide a password directly to us when using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In.

Personal Journal Data

Noma allows you to record personal moments, notes, reflections, and other journal content.

The data you may provide includes:

  • First name
  • Email address, when provided through an authentication provider
  • Journal entries and moments
  • Date and time associated with journal entries
  • Daily reminder preferences

Your journal content:

  • Is private to your account
  • Is not publicly visible
  • Is not shared with other users
  • Is not sold
  • Is not used for advertising or targeted advertising
  • Is not used to train artificial intelligence models

Noma does not require you to provide medical, health, or other sensitive information. However, because journal entries are free-form, you may choose to include sensitive or personal information in the content you write. Such content is treated as private journal data and is not intentionally analyzed for advertising, profiling, or artificial intelligence purposes.

Local Storage and Offline Use

Some data may temporarily be stored locally on your device, for example drafts or information created while your device is offline.

When connectivity is available, relevant data may be synchronized with our backend infrastructure.

Error Reporting and Diagnostics

We use Sentry to help detect application errors, crashes, and technical issues.

Sentry may process technical information such as:

  • Device and operating system information
  • Application version
  • Error messages and crash information
  • Technical identifiers
  • Diagnostic information related to application performance

We do not intentionally send the content of your personal journal entries to Sentry.

Notifications

Noma may send local notifications on your device to remind you to write in your journal.

These reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Noma does not currently use remote push notifications for daily journaling reminders.

You can enable, disable, or modify these notifications at any time from the Application settings or your device settings.

Subscriptions and Payments

Noma offers optional paid subscriptions known as Noma Plus.

Subscriptions may include:

  • A monthly subscription priced at EUR 2.99 per month
  • An annual subscription priced at EUR 23.88 per year, equivalent to EUR 1.99 per month
  • A 14-day free trial on the annual subscription, where available

Purchases are processed through Apple In-App Purchases on iOS and Google Play Billing on Android. Subscription status and entitlement management are provided through RevenueCat.

We do not directly collect, store, or process your payment card or bank account information.

Prices, billing periods, trial availability, and applicable taxes may vary depending on your country, platform, and app store.

How We Use Your Data

We use your Personal Data only as necessary to operate and provide Noma, including to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous or linked account
  • Store and synchronize your journal data
  • Allow you to access your data across supported sessions and devices
  • Remember your preferences
  • Provide subscription features
  • Diagnose crashes, errors, and technical issues
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the Application
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your Personal Data and do not use it for advertising.

Third-Party Service Providers

We rely on a limited number of third-party service providers to operate the Application.

  • Supabase: authentication and database infrastructure.
  • Sentry: crash reporting, error monitoring, and technical diagnostics.
  • RevenueCat: subscription and entitlement management.
  • Apple: authentication and In-App Purchases on iOS, where applicable.
  • Google: authentication and Google Play Billing on Android, where applicable.

These service providers may process limited information on our behalf only as necessary to provide their services.

Data Storage and International Transfers

Noma uses Supabase infrastructure located in Europe for authentication and database storage.

Some third-party service providers, such as Sentry, RevenueCat, Apple, or Google, may process certain technical, authentication, subscription, or diagnostic data outside your country or outside the European Union.

Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers of Personal Data.

Retention of Data

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application, maintain your account and journal data, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Data associated with your account is generally retained until you delete your account or request deletion, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

Deletion of Your Account and Data

You can delete your account directly from within the Application.

When you delete your account, the Personal Data associated with your Noma account is scheduled for permanent deletion. In normal circumstances, this process is immediate or completed shortly thereafter.

If any residual data cannot be deleted immediately for technical or backup-related reasons, we aim to permanently remove it within a maximum of 7 days, unless applicable law requires us to retain certain information for a longer period.

You may also contact us if you experience any issue deleting your account or Personal Data.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, including if you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have rights regarding your Personal Data, including the right to:

  • Access the Personal Data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate Personal Data
  • Request deletion of your Personal Data
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities
  • Object to certain processing activities
  • Request portability of your Personal Data where applicable
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, you may contact us using the email address provided below.

Children’s Privacy

Noma is a general-purpose journaling and note-taking application and does not impose a specific age restriction beyond any minimum age requirements that may apply under the relevant app store, platform, or applicable law.

We do not knowingly use the Application to target children or collect Personal Data from children for advertising or profiling purposes.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your Personal Data against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

No Advertising or Data Sale

Noma does not display advertising, sell your Personal Data, or use your Personal Data for targeted advertising.

Artificial Intelligence

Noma does not currently send your journal entries or other personal content to artificial intelligence services, and your content is not used to train artificial intelligence models.

If this changes in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before such processing is introduced.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Application, our service providers, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
