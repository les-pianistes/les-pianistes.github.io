Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Sunny (“the Application”) collects, uses and protects your information when you use the mobile application.

Sunny is a personal well-being journaling application. It is not a medical or health application and does not provide medical advice.

Interpretation and Definitions

Definitions

  • Application refers to Sunny, the mobile application.
  • Company (“We”, “Us”, “Our”) refers to the individual developer of Sunny.
  • Country refers to France.
  • Personal Data means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Usage Data refers to data collected automatically when using the Application.
  • You means the individual using the Application.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

Use Without Account

Sunny can be used without creating an account. In this case, your data remains associated only with your device.

Optional Account Creation

You may optionally create an account using Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In to back up and synchronize your data.

When you sign in using these services, we may receive the following information depending on your provider and settings:

  • Email address
  • First name and last name

Personal Journal Data

Sunny allows you to enter personal notes, reflections, moods and well-being related content.

This data:

  • Is private
  • Is not shared with other users
  • Is not publicly visible
  • Is not analyzed for advertising purposes

Usage Data and Analytics

With your consent, we use analytics tools (such as Mixpanel) to understand how the Application is used.

Collected analytics data may include:

  • Application events (screen views, button clicks)
  • Anonymous or pseudonymous user identifiers
  • Device and app usage information

We do not collect or analyze the content of your personal journal entries.

Analytics can be disabled at any time from the Application settings.

Notifications

Sunny may send you daily push notifications related to your journaling reminders.

You can enable, disable, or customize these notifications at any time in your device settings or within the Application.

Subscriptions and Payments

Sunny offers paid subscriptions with a free trial.

Payments are processed securely through Apple In-App Purchases. Subscription management and entitlement handling are provided via RevenueCat.

We do not store or process your payment information directly.

Retention of Data

We retain your Personal Data only for as long as necessary to provide the Application and comply with legal obligations.

Deletion of Data

You may request the deletion of your Personal Data at any time by contacting us.

Upon request, your data will be permanently deleted within a maximum of 7 days, unless legal obligations require longer retention.

Data Storage and Transfers

Your data is stored using third-party infrastructure providers (such as Supabase).

Data may be processed and stored in data centers located within or outside the European Union. Appropriate safeguards are applied to protect your data.

Children’s Privacy

Sunny is intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your Personal Data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
